You can talk through a prompt on your Mac, change your mind halfway through, and send just the question you meant to ask. With Talkie, hold your dictation shortcut while you speak. The next step depends on where you want to review your words.

## How do I dictate into ChatGPT with Talkie?

1. Install Talkie from [Downloads](/downloads/).
2. Configure your dictation hotkey. In **Settings › Dictation › Delivery**, choose **Copy & Paste** and leave **Press Enter after paste** off.
3. Open ChatGPT (browser or desktop) and click into the message field.
4. Hold the hotkey, speak the prompt, release.
5. Read the text in the message field, edit it, then send.

## Can I edit a spoken prompt before ChatGPT sees it?

In Talkie, open **Settings › Dictation › Delivery** and set **Paste action** to **Clipboard Only**. Dictate, then paste into a local text editor. If the draft is private, choose on-device transcription and turn off any cloud cleanup workflow.

**What you say aloud**

> Write Maya an email confirming Friday. Actually, she hasn’t confirmed the room yet. The door code is one two three four. Ask if twelve people will fit.

**What you send to ChatGPT**

> Draft a short email to Maya. Ask if the room seats twelve and whether Friday afternoon is available.

The second draft keeps the question and leaves out the door code. It also catches the mistake that would matter to Maya. You’re asking if Friday works.

## Does my voice go to ChatGPT?

When you use on-device transcription with cloud cleanup off, Talkie turns speech into text on your Mac. In this workflow, ChatGPT receives the words you paste and send. The recording stays out of the conversation. [How local speech recognition works](/ideas/local-speech-to-text/)
