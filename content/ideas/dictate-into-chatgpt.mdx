Dictating into ChatGPT on a Mac means speaking a prompt into the ChatGPT text box in the browser or desktop app without retyping. The reliable pattern is system-wide dictation: global hotkey, transcript, paste or insert at the focused field.

Talkie provides that Mac loop with optional local recognition and a capture library when you want history beyond a single send.

## When is voice better than typing into ChatGPT?

- Long prompts with constraints, examples, and acceptance criteria
- Thinking out loud while you still see the prior thread
- Mobile-captured context you continue on the Mac later

For short one-liners, Apple’s built-in dictation is often enough. For reusable captures and agent handoff, a dedicated dictation app fits better. Context: [Mac dictation guide](/ideas/mac-dictation/).

## How do I dictate into ChatGPT with Talkie?

1. Install Talkie from [Downloads](/downloads/).
2. Configure a global hotkey.
3. Open ChatGPT (browser or desktop) and click into the message field.
4. Hold the hotkey, speak the prompt, release.
5. Review the inserted text, then send.

If you want a cleaned prompt on the clipboard before pasting into ChatGPT, use [Dictate → Claude](/workflows/dictate-to-claude/) with the cleanup step pointed at “clear prompt, no preamble,” then paste into ChatGPT the same way.

## Local vs cloud while prompting ChatGPT

ChatGPT’s model runs in OpenAI’s cloud once you send. That does not force your **dictation** path to be cloud-based. You can still recognize speech on-device with Talkie, then paste the local transcript into ChatGPT. That split matters for drafts that should not upload as audio to a second vendor. Details: [local speech to text](/ideas/local-speech-to-text/).
