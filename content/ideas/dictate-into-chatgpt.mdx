Dictating into ChatGPT on a Mac means speaking a prompt into the ChatGPT text box in the browser or desktop app without retyping. The reliable pattern is system-wide dictation: global hotkey, transcript, paste or insert at the focused field.

Talkie provides that Mac loop with optional local recognition and a capture library when you want history beyond a single send.

## When is voice better than typing into ChatGPT?

- Long prompts with constraints, examples, and acceptance criteria
- Thinking out loud while you still see the prior thread
- Mobile-captured context you continue on the Mac later

For short one-liners, Apple’s built-in dictation is often enough. For reusable captures and agent handoff, a dedicated dictation app fits better. Context: [Mac dictation guide](/ideas/mac-dictation/).

## How do I dictate into ChatGPT with Talkie?

1. Install Talkie from [Downloads](/downloads/).
2. Configure your dictation hotkey. In **Settings › Dictation › Delivery**, choose **Copy & Paste** and leave **Press Enter after paste** off.
3. Open ChatGPT (browser or desktop) and click into the message field.
4. Hold the hotkey, speak the prompt, release.
5. Read the text in the message field, edit it, then send.

## Can I edit a spoken prompt before ChatGPT sees it?

In Talkie, open **Settings › Dictation › Delivery** and set **Paste action** to **Clipboard Only**. Dictate, then paste into a local text editor. If the draft is private, choose on-device transcription and turn off any cloud cleanup workflow.

Say you dictate, “Write Maya an email confirming Friday. Actually, she hasn’t confirmed the room yet. The door code is one two three four. Ask if twelve people will fit.”

What you paste into ChatGPT can be much shorter. “Draft a two-sentence email asking Maya whether the room seats twelve and whether Friday afternoon is available.”

Read the final question, then send it. The door code stays out of the chat, and the email asks about a date you’re still arranging.

## Local vs cloud while prompting ChatGPT

ChatGPT’s model runs in OpenAI’s cloud once you send. That does not force your **dictation** path to be cloud-based. You can still recognize speech on-device with Talkie, then paste the local transcript into ChatGPT. That split matters for drafts that should not upload as audio to a second vendor.
