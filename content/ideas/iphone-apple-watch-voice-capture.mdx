Talkie is not only a Mac dictation app. Free companion apps for iPhone and Apple Watch capture speech away from the keyboard, keep transcripts with your library, and sync through your iCloud account so the Mac can continue the work.

## Why capture on iPhone or Watch?

Useful thoughts show up while walking, between meetings, or with a wrist raise — not only at a desk. Phone Notes and voice memos work, but they often become untitled recordings you never reopen. A shared Talkie library keeps the capture searchable next to Mac dictations.

## What can you do with a mobile capture?

- **Continue on Mac** — open the memo, edit, or paste into an app
- **File as Markdown** — e.g. [voice memo → Obsidian](/workflows/voice-memo-to-obsidian/)
- **Hand to an agent** — pull via CLI or paste into Cursor / Claude ([voice remote for agents](/ideas/voice-remote-for-agents/))
- **Keep recognition local** where the mobile path allows Apple Speech or Parakeet options described on product pages

## How does sync work?

Captures start on your devices. Sync between iPhone, Watch, and Mac uses **your** iCloud account when enabled — not a mandatory Talkie cloud transcription service for core capture. Details and privacy posture: [Security](/security/) and [local speech to text](/ideas/local-speech-to-text/).

## How do I get started?

1. Install Talkie Phone from the App Store link on [Downloads](/downloads/) (Watch comes with the iPhone app where applicable).
2. Sign into the same iCloud account you use on the Mac if you want sync.
3. Record a short memo on iPhone or Watch.
4. Confirm it appears in the Mac library after sync.
5. Optionally run a workflow or dictate a follow-up on Mac ([Talkie for Mac](/mac/), [Talkie mobile](/mobile/)).
